About the book

“

Every day we make decisions that feel rational and deliberate. Many of them aren't.

“

In The Hidden Lens, Mohit Kapoor explores one of the most overlooked aspects of human behaviour: unconscious bias. He explains how it develops, why it affects the choices we make, and how greater awareness can lead to fairer decisions in our personal and professional lives.

Rather than stopping at theory, Kapoor connects bias to the situations people face every day — hiring, leadership, teamwork, the technologies we build — and offers simple, practical ways to make more thoughtful and inclusive decisions.

Awareness is where change begins. This book is written for professionals, educators, students, and curious readers alike — anyone who wants to better understand human behaviour and make fairer, more thoughtful decisions.

What the book covers

Rather than looking at unconscious bias from a single perspective, the book examines it from many angles. It explores:

How unconscious
bias develops

The neuroscience of pattern-matching — and why it isn’t a moral failing.

on shortcuts

The cognitive load equation and how shortcuts became survival tools.

03

What shapes our
assumptions

Upbringing, culture, media, and the invisible curriculum we all absorb.

04

How bias influences
decisions at work

From hiring and promotions to the meetings you don’t get invited to.

05

Practical ways to reduce bias

Everyday habits, team rituals, and structural fixes that actually stick.

06

Bias and emerging
technologies

What happens when biased data trains models we then trust to be neutral.

07

Why self-awareness is hard

The paradox at the heart of introspection — and how to work around it.
