India's First Laserpreneur®

In 2008, Dr. Prof. Yavagal's father was diagnosed with cancer that had spread to the spine. Chemotherapy and radiation left him with severe mouth ulcers. Eating hurt. Speaking became difficult. Relief was limited.

Within the year, Dr. Prof. Yavagal lost his father.

That helplessness became a question: if light could reduce pain, support healing, and restore dignity, why was photomedicine still inaccessible to so many? Why should Indian doctors depend on imported systems they could not afford?
